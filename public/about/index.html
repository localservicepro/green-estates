Our story

A local lawn and garden business, built on turning up

Green Estates Gardening is owned and operated by Hamish Baggley from Wamuran, Queensland. The business provides lawn mowing, acreage mowing, hedge trimming, garden cleanups, weed control, mulching and minor tree work across the Moreton Bay and Somerset regions, with fixed-price quotes and a 5.0 Google rating.

Ask anyone who has tried to find a gardener in Caboolture or Morayfield what the hardest part is and they will tell you: getting someone to show up twice. Green Estates was built around that one problem. Every job is quoted as a fixed price, every visit goes in the calendar, and the same people come back each time so the standard never slips.

Hamish Baggley has spent more than 13 years mowing, hedging and clearing properties across South-East Queensland, from tight townhouse courtyards in North Lakes to 20-acre blocks in the Somerset valleys. That experience shows in the small things: knowing when a buffalo lawn wants to be cut higher, which hedges will not reshoot from old wood, and how a Wamuran paddock behaves after a week of February rain.

What local gardeners Moreton Bay property owners can expect from us

  • Reliable: we come on the day we said, and we message when the job is done.
  • Honest pricing: one fixed price, agreed before we start, no hourly surprises.
  • Properly finished: edged, trimmed, blown down and green waste removed every time.
  • Genuinely local: based in Wamuran, working across Moreton Bay and Somerset every week.

FAQ

About Green Estates Gardening: common questions

Who will actually turn up to do the work?

Hamish Baggley, the owner, with the same small local team on every visit. We do not subcontract rounds out, which is why the standard is consistent from Redcliffe to Kilcoy.

Where are you based and how far do you travel?

We are based in Wamuran, north-west of Caboolture. Regular rounds cover the Moreton Bay Region from Redcliffe and North Lakes up through Morayfield and Caboolture to Woodford and D'Aguilar, and the Somerset Region including Kilcoy, Esk, Toogoolawah, Lowood and Fernvale.

Are you a registered business?

Yes. We trade as Green Estates Gardening Pty Ltd and invoice every job. Details are on every quote and invoice.

Do you offer regular maintenance packages?

Yes, and most clients end up on one. A package bundles lawn mowing with seasonal hedge trimming, weed control and a spring mulch under one fixed monthly or per-visit price, scheduled around your grass and your season.
